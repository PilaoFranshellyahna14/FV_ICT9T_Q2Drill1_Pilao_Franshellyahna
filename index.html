🏸A Beginner's Guide to Badminton🏸



Basic Rules

  • Serve diagonally> across the net
  • The serve must be underhand
  • Play best of 3 games to 21 points
  • Winner: First to win 2 of 3 games (singles or doubles)

Equipment

  • Badminton Racket
  • Shuttlecock aka Birdie
  • Net and Posts
  • Appropriate Shoes

Techniques

  • Forehand and Backhand Shots
  • Good Footwork
  • Smash Attack
  • Singles: Solo/Doubles: Teamwork
